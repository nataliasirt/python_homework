3. Why do websites use robots.txt and how does it promote ethical scraping? 
Websites use robots.txt to guide crawlers on accessible site areas, managing server load, protecting sensitive content, and ensuring relevant pages are indexed. It promotes ethical scraping by clearly communicating the site owner’s preferences, encouraging crawlers to respect these boundaries.
